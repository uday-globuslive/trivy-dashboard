Risk Score Calculation
Methodology

The risk score is calculated based on the vulnerability severity distribution in the latest scan of this project. It uses a weighted severity scoring system that normalizes the total weighted score to a 0-100% scale.

Severity Weights
  • Critical = 10 points
  • High = 7 points
  • Medium = 4 points
  • Low = 1 point
Formula

Risk Score % = (Total Weighted Score ÷ Max Possible Score) × 100

Where:
• Total Weighted Score = (Critical × 10) + (High × 7) + (Medium × 4) + (Low × 1)
• Max Possible Score = Total Vulnerabilities × 10

Risk Levels

CRITICAL 80-100%

HIGH 60-79%

MEDIUM 40-59%

LOW 20-39%

MINIMAL 0-19%

Score is updated after each Trivy scan completes
