Risk Score Calculation
Methodology

The risk score is calculated based on the vulnerability severity distribution in this scan. It uses a weighted severity scoring system that normalizes the total weighted score to a 0-100% scale.

Severity Weights
  • Critical = 10 points
  • High = 7 points
  • Medium = 4 points
  • Low = 1 point
Formula

Risk Score % = (Total Weighted Score ÷ Max Possible Score) × 100

Where:
• Total Weighted Score = (Critical × 10) + (High × 7) + (Medium × 4) + (Low × 1)
• Max Possible Score = Total Vulnerabilities × 10
